# AI Matome Graphic Style V2 - Current / Default

## Reference Analysis

The V2 references share a contemporary 3D profile-avatar language rather than a single character template. Human, animal, bird, plush animal, creature, and object-inspired characters can coexist when they follow the same circular-avatar portrait system.

- Character proportion: close bust or iconic portrait crop, with more intentional head-to-shoulder or head-to-body relationships than V1.
- Face: simplified dot/oval eyes, short brows, small mouth marks, and occasional minimal nose marks.
- Head-to-body ratio: graphically emphasized heads are allowed, but avoid baby-like oversized heads on tiny bodies.
- Human vs non-human treatment: humans use contemporary bust portrait styling; animals, birds, plush forms, and creatures use iconic ears, snouts, beaks, tails, crests, or rounded bodies while staying profile-system-ready rather than preschool mascot-like.
- Hair: sculpted contemporary masses such as bobs, waves, caps-over-hair, bangs, tied hair, or cropped shapes.
- Fashion / accessories: one distinctive cue such as glasses, sunglasses, cap, beanie, headphones, collar, jacket, knitwear, bow tie, small hat, animal feature, or compact held object.
- Material / texture: controlled material variation across skin, sculpted hair, fabric, denim, knit, soft fur, plush, matte beaks/snouts, rubbery creature skin, and glossy lenses.
- 3D modeling characteristics: softened clean forms, broad readable planes, clear silhouettes, moderate volume.
- Lighting: soft studio lighting with gentle shadows and broad highlights.
- Rendering: polished stylized 3D, not photorealistic and not generic Pixar-like.
- Composition / crop: square 1:1, circular-avatar-safe, close portrait framing; front, slight three-quarter, and simple side-profile views are all valid.
- Background: clean pastel or soft solid background with no scenery or graphic clutter.
- Color treatment: natural and grounded tones plus one or two controlled accents; not uniformly candy-pastel.
- Expression: calm, curious, confident, witty, relaxed, or lightly warm; cute only when conceptually appropriate.
- Level of detail: medium-low, with enough styling/material specificity to feel contemporary.

## V1 / V2 Comparison

| Item | V1 - Legacy Graphic Style | V2 - Current / Default Graphic Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall Tone | Playful, toy-like, cute mascot | Mature, contemporary, editorial 3D avatar |
| Character Proportion | Head-dominant mascot with tiny/simple body | Mature bust portrait or iconic non-human portrait with less chibi bias |
| Face | Very simple graphic marks, often cute or mascot-like | Simple dot/line face, but calmer, more confident, less juvenile |
| Material | Mostly quiet matte/satin toy surfaces with rare exceptions | Controlled material diversity: skin, hair, fabric, denim, knit, fur, plush, lenses |
| Styling | Identity often carried by mascot/object hybrid and one toy-like motif | Identity carried by silhouette, contemporary styling, and one distinctive feature |
| Detail | Very low detail, broad toy geometry | Medium-low detail with selective material and fashion cues |
| Agent Expression | Friendly, playful, charming mascot expression | Curious, composed, witty, confident, relaxed, or lightly warm |
| Avoid | Photorealism, anime, clutter, detailed surfaces | All V1 avoids plus overly childish, baby/chibi, generic cute mascot, excessive blush, glossy plastic, costume-like storytelling |

## Expanded V2 Form Library

The V2 form library now includes duck, rabbit, mole, penguin, bear, cockatoo, hamster, alien, cat, dog, puppy, and human bust variants. These references expand the allowed silhouette vocabulary; they are not characters to copy one-to-one.

- Animal and bird forms: use ears, snouts, beaks, tails, crests, collars, bow ties, or small hats as the main silhouette cue.
- Plush forms: soft fur and rounded bodies are allowed when they remain clean, restrained, and profile-avatar-safe.
- Human forms: use hair silhouette, eyewear, headwear, headphones, facial marks, and cropped shoulders to create identity.
- Shared rule: preserve a clean circular crop, simple face marks, soft studio 3D rendering, pastel/soft background, and one dominant feature.
